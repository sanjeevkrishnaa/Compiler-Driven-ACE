> **Interpretation boundary.** The workload, trace ordering, seeds, four-memory shared pool, compiler/autonomous cap of three, and demand-first admission rule are matched. Absolute milliseconds are therefore shown for reproducibility, but only paired comparisons *within one backend* are scientific comparisons.
